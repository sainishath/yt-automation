# Monetization Safety & Compliance Specification

---

## 🛡️ 1. Anti-Repetition & Authentic Originality Standards

YouTube policies strictly penalize programmatic, mass-produced, or low-value repetitious content. We enforce five architectural safeguards:

1. **Academic RAG Grounding:** Scripts synthesize verifiable dates, historical figures, and causal mechanisms rather than generic LLM hallucinations.
2. **0 Unsupported Claims:** Scripts pass an automated claim verification audit before audio synthesis.
3. **Contextual Visual Prompting:** Every visual beat is individually prompted with accurate wardrobe, architectural, and lighting details.
4. **Dynamic Speech Timing:** Narration lengths and scene transitions derive directly from Whisper speech durations (never mechanical fixed grids).
5. **Mandatory Human Discord Review Gate:** 100% of generated videos require explicit human review and approval prior to publishing.

---

## 🤖 2. Generative AI Disclosure Compliance

All uploads automatically pass synthetic media disclosure tags to comply with YouTube's Altered/Synthetic Media disclosure guidelines:
```text
[Altered or Synthetic Content: This video contains AI-generated visual and audio content.]
```

---

## ⚖️ 3. Copyright & Advertiser-Friendly Standards

- Background music tracks are royalty-free/custom acoustic stems ducked to ambient levels (-22 dB to -26 dB).
- Visual assets are uniquely generated via local SDXL (no scraped copyrighted video clips).
- Sensitive topics avoid sensationalism, violence, hate speech, or deceptive clickbait.
