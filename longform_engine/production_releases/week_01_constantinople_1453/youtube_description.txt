The 53 Days That Shattered Rome: The Fall of Constantinople (1453)

In 1453, fifty-three days of brutal siege ended a millennium of Roman imperial civilization. From the 26-foot Basilic supercannons of master founder Urban to the overland transport of 70 warships into the Golden Horn, this documentary traces the collapse of the Theodosian Walls and the dawn of the modern world.
