# Independent Channel Strategy Specification

## 🏛️ CHANNEL A: *Chronos Shift* (Pipeline 1: Alternate History)
- **Handle:** `@ChronosShiftAI`
- **Core Positioning:** Cinematic Alternate History & Turning Points of Civilization.
- **Target Audience:** Ages 18–35; history enthusiasts, strategy gamers (*Paradox*, *Civilization*), sci-fi speculative fiction readers.
- **Content Pillars:**
  1. Fallen Empires & Divergent Battles (Rome, Mongols, Spanish Armada).
  2. Lost Knowledge & Ancient Technology (Alexandria, Antikythera, Greek steam power).
  3. 20th Century Near-Misses (Cold War, Space Race, Nuclear Brinkmanship).
  4. Evolutionary & Prehistoric What-Ifs (Neanderthals, Dinosaur survival).
- **Posting Cadence:** 3–4 Shorts per week (Mon, Wed, Fri, Sat at 10:00 AM EST).
- **Production Engine:** Pipeline 1 (`alternate-history-shorts`) with Candidate A linear Ken Burns camera motion and RAG v4 academic grounding.
