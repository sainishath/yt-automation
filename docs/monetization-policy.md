# YouTube Monetization & Compliance Policy

---

## 🏛️ 1. YouTube Partner Program (YPP) Requirements

To achieve and sustain monetization on YouTube, channels must meet the official YouTube Partner Program eligibility thresholds and comply with all Community Guidelines, Terms of Service, and AdSense Program Policies.

### Eligibility Criteria (2026 Baseline)
- **Shorts Monetization Pathway:**
  - 1,000 Subscribers **AND**
  - 10 Million valid public Shorts views within the last 90 days.
- **Standard Video Pathway (Compilations / Long-Form):**
  - 1,000 Subscribers **AND**
  - 4,000 valid public Watch Hours within the last 12 months.
- **Safety Compliance:**
  - Active 2-Step Verification on Google Account.
  - Follow Community Guidelines (0 active strikes).
  - Linked active AdSense account.

YouTube strictly penalizes channels that produce programmatic, mass-generated, or low-value repetitive content. We implement five concrete architectural safeguards:

1. **Substantive Educational & Narrative Value:**  
   Scripts are not generic summaries; they synthesize specific dates, historical figures, and causal mechanisms grounded in RAG evidence packets.
2. **Dynamic Audio Pacing:**  
   Narration uses natural speech timing and punctuation pauses, avoiding robotic monotone cadence.
3. **Photorealistic Context-Specific Assets:**  
   Every visual frame is individually prompted using extracted visual evidence (clothing, architecture, lighting) rather than recycled generic stock photos.
4. **Editorial Review:**  
   No video is ever auto-published without an explicit human review and approval in Discord.

## 🤖 3. Synthetic & Altered Media Disclosure Compliance

Under YouTube's updated generative AI guidelines, creators are required to disclose when content has been meaningfully altered or synthetically generated by AI.

### Disclosure Requirements
- **Visuals:** SDXL imagery depicting realistic scenes of historical figures or events is flagged as synthetic media.
- **Audio:** Neural text-to-speech audio is disclosed.
- **Implementation in Pipelines:**
  1. **Video Description Tagging:** Every uploaded video includes the explicit disclosure:  
     `[Altered or Synthetic Content: This video contains AI-generated visual and audio content.]`
  2. **Metadata API Declaration:** `upload_video.py` and `uploader.py` pass clean metadata and appropriate category tags (`Category 27: Education`).

## ⚖️ 4. Copyright & Advertiser-Friendly Safety

1. **Audio Rights:** Background music is ducked to ambient levels (-22 dB to -26 dB) and uses only royalty-free/public-domain audio or custom acoustic stems.
2. **No Misleading Claims:** RAG historical grounding ensures counterfactuals are explicitly framed as *"What-If"* scenarios, avoiding deceptive clickbait or historical disinformation.
3. **Zero Advertiser-Blacklisted Tropes:** Prompts strictly avoid excessive violence, hate speech, dangerous activities, or copyrighted character trademarks.
