## 🏛️ PART 1: Channel Positioning & Audience Analysis

| Attribute | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Core Theme** | Cinematic Alternate History & Turning Points of Civilization |
| **Primary Audience** | Ages 18–35; history buffs, speculative fiction readers, strategy gamers (Paradox, Civilization), geography & geopolitical trivia fans |

1. **Fallen Empires & Divergent Battles:** *What if Rome never fell? What if the Spanish Armada conquered England? What if the Mongols took Vienna?*
2. **Lost Knowledge & Tech Leaps:** *What if the Library of Alexandria survived? What if the steam engine was adopted in Ancient Greece?*
3. **20th Century Near-Misses:** *What if the Cuban Missile Crisis escalated? What if the internet was invented in 1950?*
4. **Geographical & Evolutionary What-Ifs:** *What if the Mediterranean Sea never reopened? What if Neanderthals coexisted with modern civilization?*

## 📈 PART 2: 90-Day Content Roadmap & Release Cadence

### Month 1: Baseline Testing & Audience Seeding (Weeks 1–4)
- **Cadence:**
  - **Channel A:** 3 Shorts per week (Mon, Wed, Fri at 10:00 AM EST).
  - **Channel B:** 5 Shorts per week (Mon through Fri at 3:00 PM EST).
- **Core Focus:**
  - Establish algorithmic baseline across 4 core content pillars.
  - Test 3 distinct hook frameworks per pillar (Negative Bias vs Open Loop vs Counter-Intuitive Claim).
  - Track **Viewed vs Swiped Away Rate** (Goal: $\ge 75\%$) and **Average Percentage Viewed** (Goal: $\ge 85\%$).

### Month 2: Format Winner Selection & Recurring Franchises (Weeks 5–8)
- **Cadence:**
  - **Channel A:** 4 Shorts per week (introducing a Saturday "Empire Special").
  - **Channel B:** 6 Shorts per week.
- **Core Focus:**
  - Kill underperforming pillars ($< 65\%$ viewed rate).
  - Double down on top-performing themes (e.g., Roman Empire or Ancient Tech).
  - Launch named recurring mini-series:
    * Channel A: *"Turning Points That Broke History"* (Part 1 to 5).
    * Channel B: *"Brain Glitches You Can't Control"* (Part 1 to 5).
  - Introduce pinned comment debate loops to increase comment engagement by $> 30\%$.

### Month 3: Audience Consolidation & Long-Form Expansion (Weeks 9–12)
- **Cadence:**
  - **Channel A:** 4 Shorts/week + **1 Long-Form Compilation (8–12 min)** every 2 weeks.
  - **Channel B:** 6 Shorts/week + **1 Deep-Dive Video Essay (10 min)** per month.
- **Core Focus:**
  - Package top 5 related Shorts into themed long-form documentaries with unified narration.
  - Build long-form public watch hours to accelerate YPP eligibility.
  - Establish community tab polls asking viewers to vote on next week's counterfactual timeline or debate topic.

## 🎯 PART 3: Hook & Retention Architecture (Score $\ge 8.5/10$)

Every generated script must pass the 8-point Hook & Narrative Pacing rubric:

```text
Score:  1-3 = REJECT  |  4-5 = WEAK  |  6-7 = ACCEPTABLE  |  8 = STRONG  |  9-10 = EXCEPTIONAL
```

### Pipeline 1: 8-Beat Counterfactual Arc
1. **Beat 0 (0.0–4.5s) — High-Tension Hook:** Active counterfactual premise (e.g., *"If Ptolemy XIII hadn't burned the Library of Alexandria, humanity would have reached the moon by 1400."*).
2. **Beat 1 (4.5–9.5s) — The Pivot Point:** The exact historical moment of divergence.
3. **Beat 2 (9.5–15.0s) — Immediate Consequence:** What changed on day one.
4. **Beat 3 (15.0–21.0s) — Escalation:** Geopolitical ripple effects across neighboring civilizations.
5. **Beat 4 (21.0–27.5s) — The Cultural/Scientific Shift:** Technology or society transforming.
6. **Beat 5 (27.5–34.0s) — Unexpected Dark Implication:** The unforeseen cost or danger of this alternate reality.
7. **Beat 6 (34.0–40.5s) — Modern Day Climax:** What our present world looks like today.
8. **Beat 7 (40.5–46.0s) — Comment Trigger:** *"Would you choose this timeline, or did history get it right? Comment below."*

### Pipeline 2: Two-Host Clash Arc
1. **Hook (0.0–4.0s):** Provocative attack on a common habit or intuition.
2. **Speaker A (4.0–12.0s):** Asserts the controversial premise with an extreme claim.
3. **Speaker B (12.0–20.0s):** Challenges with skepticism, demanding mechanism or proof.
4. **Speaker A (20.0–28.0s):** Reveals the underlying neuroscience or paradox mechanism.
5. **Speaker B (28.0–35.0s):** Concedes or offers an even more shocking counter-twist.
6. **Outro (35.0–42.0s):** Punchy punchline and natural comment debate trigger.

## 📊 PART 4: Metric Scorecard (Keep vs Modify vs Kill)

| Metric | Target | Action if Above | Action if Below |
|---|:---:|---|---|
| **Viewed vs Swiped Away** | $\ge 75\%$ | Expand topic into a 3-part series | Reject hook structure; test 3 alternative Scene 0 lines |
| **Average Percentage Viewed (APV)** | $\ge 85\%$ | Re-use narrative pacing & beat durations | Identify drop-off timestamp; cut filler words in that beat |
| **Comment-to-View Ratio** | $\ge 0.5\%$ | Pin top comment debate; engage with replies | Add a direct polar question in the outro beat |
| **Subscriber Conversion** | $\ge 1 : 500$ | Feature channel branding in top-left corner | Add subtle call-to-subscribe in description metadata |
